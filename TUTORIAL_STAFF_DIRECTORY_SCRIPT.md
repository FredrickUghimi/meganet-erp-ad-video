## Where the screens come from
Every captured screen is a real capture of **EHR-Cloud- origin/main `e8ca779` (APP_VERSION 1.94.0) running locally**: Postgres 16, `seed_mega_net_demo` (the fictional "Mega-Net Demo Hospital" organisation), Django console email backend (no email is ever sent), headless Chromium at a 360 CSS px viewport and device scale 3 (`scripts/tutorials/capture_*.js`). **Nothing was captured from staging, production or the Northgate demo; no real data or real email address appears.** Names and `.example` addresses are fictional; "Nneka Eze" was created by the capture script. Each frame carries the chip "Fictional demo data". The delete confirmation (a native browser dialog that screenshots cannot capture), the role list (a native dropdown) and the title / steps / end cards are **drawn, not captured**, and labelled so on screen. The Staff Directory screens at 360 px show the app's own demo banner only in cropped-out areas.

The invite toast shows only "Nneka Eze invited": the words after "invited" are painted over with the toast's own colour and the lines below are cut away (`scripts/tutorials/tutorial_assets.py`: inside the painted area the pixels equal the text-less capture, max difference 0; the kept words still differ from it).

## Wording rules (project owner, 2026-11-17)
- No narration or caption claims that an email, invite or link was delivered or received. Tutorial 1 describes the invite step only; it says the account is created and that there is no password field ("you never choose or see a password"). Delivery is unverified (see "Facts the project owner needs").
- Deactivate means "removes their access to this organization; nothing is deleted".
- Nothing about security, encryption or compliance.
- Not taught: editing a deactivated person (Save silently reactivates them), and what a changed role allows.

## Facts the project owner needs
**App issues found while building (suggestions, none fixed here; `EHR-Cloud-` was not touched):**
1. The disabled **Deactivate** button is white on pale red, **2.48:1** (`frontend/src/pages/StaffDirectory.jsx:267` disables it with no row selected or on a deactivated row).
2. On a phone-width screen (360 px) the **selected-row** card draws a heavy blue outline over the first letters of every field (worst pixel 2.95:1; the text itself is 12.8:1).
3. The footer link **"Need help? Chat with us on WhatsApp"** is teal on a pale background, **3.70:1** (4.14:1 on white).
4. Saving an edit on a **deactivated person silently reactivates them** (`api/staff_directory.py:138` sets `is_active = True`), and there is **no Reactivate button**.
5. The **invite toast cannot prove delivery**: it says "a password-setup email was sent to ..." whether or not the send worked (`api/staff_directory.py:163-166`). Django swallows send failures (`django/contrib/auth/forms.py:433-438`), so the API still answers 201. Confirmed locally with a backend that always fails.
Also: the demo banner text is 4.47:1 at its worst pixel (4.55:1 typical), and the page title is truncated to "Sta..." at 360 px.

**Production email** (names only, values never read; `meganet/settings.py:414-436`, `render.yaml`): `EMAIL_HOST_PASSWORD` (the Resend API key, the only variable that turns real sending on; blank = console backend, the email incl. its set-password link prints to the Render Logs), `DEFAULT_FROM_EMAIL` (default is Resend's shared sandbox sender, which per the code comments delivers only to the Resend account owner until a domain is verified), `EMAIL_BACKEND` (optional override). `EMAIL_HOST`, `EMAIL_PORT`, `EMAIL_HOST_USER`, `EMAIL_USE_TLS` are dormant under the Resend backend. `render.yaml` declares none of them, so they live only in Render's dashboard and could not be checked from this environment.

## Deliberately not claimed
That an invite email was sent or received; anything about production or staging behaviour; what a role can do after it is changed; that deactivation affects the person's account at any other organization (it is membership-only by design, `foundation/views.py:873-880`); sorting (the table header is hidden at 360 px); that the Staff Directory is hidden from every non-admin role (only the nurse role was tested; the other six are code-read: `foundation/migrations/0012_seed_staff_management_permission.py:16-18`).
